Farkle Score v1.3.0

Release highlights:
- Players roll a physical die and select the player who rolled highest.
- Random first-player selection remains available.
- Same Players returns to the Choose Who Goes First screen.
- Winner screen includes Congratulations, animated balloons, and all other final scores in one row.
- Large Straight uses a fuchsia background.
- Three Pairs uses a silver-gray background.
- Three 1s, Three 2s, and Three 3s progress from lightest to darkest blue.
- New Players button on the winner screen uses a pink background.
- Increased touch targets and touch-response styling for iPad use.
- App, package, and service-worker cache versions updated to v1.3.0.
